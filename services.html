Common Questions

Before you sign up

Can I switch disciplines mid-membership?

Yes — Competitive and Elite members can switch or add a discipline at any monthly renewal with no extra sign-up fee.

Do I need my own equipment?

Core equipment is provided for every discipline. Personal gear (boots, racquets, etc.) is recommended once you move past the trial session.

Is physiotherapy included in every plan?

Physio is bundled into Competitive and Elite plans. Recreational members can book pay-per-session physio at member rates.

What's the cancellation policy?

All plans are month-to-month with no lock-in — cancel anytime from your member dashboard before the next billing cycle.

Not sure which plan fits?

Talk to a coach — we'll recommend a program based on your goals and schedule.
